Codebook · four form-based categories

Application order

Test the endings in the order shown below. Stop at the first match. This rule prevents one term from belonging to several categories. Category names are analytical labels, not final dictionary senses.

The codebook operationalizes nominal-to-verbal translation through English morphology. It is informed by Halliday and Martin’s discussion of grammatical metaphor and nominalization (1993), and by Vinay and Darbelnet’s concept of transposition (1958/1995). Those theories explain why a grammatical category can change across languages; the present categories deliberately use a narrower, reproducible feature.

Four known lexical words whose spelling happens to end in a tested suffix are excluded from the derivational categories: chance, dance, romance, and trance. They remain lexical/root-coded nouns.

套用次序

按以下次序檢查字尾，遇到第一個符合的規則便停止。這樣可避免同一詞語落入多個類別。類別名稱是分析標籤，不是最終的詞典義項。

本分類手冊透過英文構詞特徵來操作「名詞轉動詞」問題，理論上參考 Halliday 與 Martin 對語法隱喻及名詞化的討論（1993），以及 Vinay 與 Darbelnet 的詞類轉換概念（1958/1995）。這些理論說明跨語言時語法類別為何可能改變；本分類則刻意採用較窄且可重現的詞形特徵。

四個詞語的拼寫剛好以測試字尾結束，但並非本規則要處理的派生詞： chance、dance、romance、trance。 它們保留在詞根式名詞。

1. Action / process nominal

Definition
An English noun ending in -tion or -sion, prototypically derived from a verb and naming an act, process, or its abstract framing.
Theory source
Halliday and Martin (1993) on nominalization; Vinay and Darbelnet (1958/1995) on transposition.
Decision rule
If the lowercased key noun ends in -tion or -sion, assign this category before testing any other ending.
Positive example
attention → 注意.
Negative example
dance → 跳舞 has no tested derivational ending and belongs to the lexical/root category.
Boundary case
position fits formally even where a particular context names a physical location. The category describes form first, so semantic readings are kept in the retrospective notes rather than overriding the rule.

1. 動作／過程名詞化

定義
以 -tion 或 -sion 結尾的英文名詞，典型上由動詞派生，用來命名動作、過程或對它們的抽象概括。
理論來源
Halliday 與 Martin（1993）的名詞化討論；Vinay 與 Darbelnet（1958/1995）的詞類轉換概念。
判斷規則
若小寫關鍵名詞以 -tion 或 -sion 結尾，先於其他字尾檢查並歸入此類。
正面例子
attention → 注意。
反面例子
dance → 跳舞 沒有上述測試字尾，應歸入詞根式名詞。
邊界例子
即使某些語境中的 position 指實際位置，它在形式上仍符合本類。類別先描述詞形，因此語意觀察留給補充說明，而不推翻可重現的規則。

2. Result / product nominal

Definition
An English noun ending in -ment that is not already covered by the preceding action/process rule. It often names a result, arrangement, payment, or institutional product.
Theory source
Halliday and Martin (1993) on nominalization; Vinay and Darbelnet (1958/1995) on transposition.
Decision rule
After excluding -tion and -sion, assign nouns ending in -ment to this category.
Positive example
payment → 付款.
Negative example
protection → 保护 belongs to action/process because -tion has priority.
Boundary case
appointment can mean both an arrangement and an event. The first-match rule keeps it in this category; the uncertainty is acknowledged rather than hidden.

2. 結果／成品名詞化

定義
未被前一項動作／過程規則涵蓋、以 -ment 結尾的英文名詞。這類詞常用來命名結果、安排、付款或制度成品。
理論來源
Halliday 與 Martin（1993）的名詞化討論；Vinay 與 Darbelnet（1958/1995）的詞類轉換概念。
判斷規則
先排除 -tion 和 -sion，再把以 -ment 結尾的名詞歸入此類。
正面例子
payment → 付款。
反面例子
protection → 保护 因 -tion 優先而歸入動作／過程。
邊界例子
appointment 可指安排，也可指事件。先到先得規則把它留在本類，並明示這種不確定性。

3. State / quality nominal

Definition
An English noun ending in -ance or -ence after the earlier endings have been excluded. The form often relates to a condition, quality, or continuing state.
Theory source
Halliday and Martin (1993) on grammatical metaphor and nominalization.
Decision rule
If no earlier category matched, the noun is not one of the four listed lexical exceptions, and it ends in -ance or -ence, assign this category.
Positive example
obedience → 服从.
Negative example
destruction → 毁灭 belongs to action/process because it ends in -tion.
Boundary case
performance can name an action as well as a quality. Its ending places it here for now; this semantic mismatch is an explicit reason for later revision after a human readability test.

3. 狀態／性質名詞化

定義
在排除先前字尾後，以 -ance 或 -ence 結尾的英文名詞。這類詞形常與狀態、性質或持續情況有關。
理論來源
Halliday 與 Martin（1993）的語法隱喻及名詞化討論。
判斷規則
若未符合較早類別、詞語不在上述四個例外中，且關鍵名詞以 -ance 或 -ence 結尾，便歸入此類。
正面例子
obedience → 服从。
反面例子
destruction → 毁灭 以 -tion 結尾，應歸入動作／過程。
邊界例子
performance 可指動作，也可指性質表現。目前按字尾歸入本類；這種語意落差正是日後人工可讀性測試後修訂規則的理由。

4. Lexical / root-coded noun

Definition
An English key noun that does not end in one of the tested suffixes. This category is intentionally residual and records lexical or zero-marked nominal forms.
Theory source
Vinay and Darbelnet (1958/1995) on transposition, where source and target may not share the same grammatical class.
Decision rule
Assign only after the word fails all three suffix tests.
Positive example
dance → 跳舞.
Negative example
silence → 闭嘴 ends in -ence and therefore belongs to state/quality under the formal rule.
Boundary case
capture behaves semantically like an action, yet it has no tested suffix and therefore stays here. The codebook measures form, not speaker intuition.

4. 詞根式名詞

定義
不以任何測試字尾結尾的英文關鍵名詞。此類刻意保留為餘項，用來記錄詞根形式或零形式名詞。
理論來源
Vinay 與 Darbelnet（1958/1995）的詞類轉換概念；原文與譯文不必屬於相同詞類。
判斷規則
只有在詞語未通過前三項字尾測試時才歸入此類。
正面例子
dance → 跳舞。
反面例子
silence → 闭嘴 以 -ence 結尾，按形式規則歸入狀態／性質。
邊界例子
capture 在語意上像動作，但沒有測試字尾，因此仍留在此類。本手冊衡量詞形，不衡量說話者的直覺。
